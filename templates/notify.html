Setting it up

Two steps, once, about three minutes. Telegram bots are free and there is nothing to register — the bot you make here belongs to you and talks only to you.

  1. 1

    Make a bot and paste its token

    In Telegram, open @BotFather and send it /newbot. It asks for a name and a username, then answers with a token that looks like 7123456789:AAF-a1b2c3…. Paste that whole line here.

    The token is written to this instance's data volume and never leaves this machine except to talk to Telegram. It is never printed back into this page — only its bot number and last four characters are shown above, so a screenshot of this page gives nothing away. {%- if telegram.token_from_container %} The token in force came from the container's TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN; saving one here replaces it.{% endif %} Anyone holding this token can post as your bot, so treat it like a password: if it leaks, send /revoke to @BotFather and paste the new one here.

  2. 2

    Tell the bot who to write to

    Open your new bot in Telegram — @BotFather links to it — and send it any message at all. That single message is what lets it reply: Telegram never allows a bot to write first. Then press the button below and this app reads your chat id straight off the bot.

What arrives, and when

The message is written for a phone, not for this page.

One message, not one per job
Daily runs run one after another as the day's credits allow. The report goes out once they have all finished, listing every job that ran, what it added, what is left, and what it cost. Three jobs are one message, because to you they are one event.
Nothing runs, nothing is sent
A quiet day produces no message at all. There is no “nothing happened” ping to train yourself to ignore, and no message when you start a playlist mirror by hand — you are already watching that one.
A failed message never breaks a playlist mirror
If Telegram cannot be reached, the videos are still copied and the job still holds its state; only the report is lost, and the reason for it is printed on this page. A mirror's outcome never depends on a chat app being up.
